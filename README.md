# 📚 AI, Generative AI, Data Science & Data Engineering — Master's-Level Reading Roadmap

A curated reading roadmap for students and professionals preparing for **Master's-level study and careers in Artificial Intelligence, Generative AI, Machine Learning, Data Science, Data Engineering, and AI Engineering**.

The roadmap is inspired by topics commonly covered in graduate-level programs at universities such as **University of Pennsylvania, Columbia University, and the University of California system**.
